## Inside the edit

**Rhythm.** One event on every stressed word, every 0.8–1.5 s. Every beat also moves the camera (push-in or 3D shake). Pauses are cut ("Cut to whole phrases / Cut the pauses").

**The signature: word behind the head.** Huge word, 1 per phrase, sits on the top of the head so the hair covers its lower part. Three looks:
- realtor style: Inter-like heavy sans, light-blue → blue gradient, tiny white kicker above ("recently / **sold**", "the grand / **foyer**")
- podcast style: white-metal serif italic ("**Sold**", "**Over**", "**Whisper**"), or tall condensed caps ("**DEGREE**", "**EMPIRE**")
- lime brand style: serif italic in lime on a film strip / ribbon ("*editors*", "*Personal*")

**Cards on the words** (glass white, rounded 30, soft shadow, spring in from below):
app card with thumbnail + green ✓ badge ("The house ✓ Sold"), price/offer card with a bar filling ("Multiple offers"), stat ("TOTAL LISTINGS 165" counting up), checklist pills ticking ("✓ New restaurants ✓ Brand new brewery"), chat bubble ("Thinking about selling?"), slider/search bar, star rating, "Going *viral*" card.

**Depth tricks** (person cutout above the graphics): glowing arc/halo around the body, photos orbiting and passing behind, glass ribbon wrapping the body, photos popping out from behind the shoulders, dot globe / dot world map / dot path behind the head, light beams behind ("light scene on the punch line").

**Обводки (outlines).** The cutout gets a contour: a white neon tube with a coloured glow that flickers on and draws head → feet (hook, "sold", the number), a thick white sticker edge with a drop shadow (when photos fly around — reads like a collage), or a soft coloured rim light (podcast, calm talk). Plus the drawn shapes around the body: glowing arc (`halo`), glass ribbon (`ribbon`). Engine: `outline` = a second copy of the cutout under the real one, grown with stacked CSS drop-shadows.

**Перебивки (title cards).** Between two thoughts the speaker disappears for 0.6–1 s: dark pleated curtain, a diagonal glass light, small caps kicker ("SO I'M MORE", "IF YOU'RE TALKING ABOUT") or serif kicker ("field of"), then a huge metal condensed word ("NUANCED NOW", "ENTREPRENEURS", "VIEW"), one glowing blue dot — or a dashed path with labelled glowing nodes ("1A → 1B"). Enters and leaves with a whip blur. Refs: Df447Vfj36j 9 s, Df65XGeD-V0 2.6 s, Df6tcTSD5Cw 1.6 s / 6.5 s.

**Cutaways.** AI B-roll (Gemini/Flow) 0.5–1 s on a concrete noun ("motivation" → runner on stairs, "Nvidia" → glowing chip), entered with a whip blur; a full-screen dark card with a line chart on numbers ("And when *yours* don't").

**Captions.** Not karaoke pills: a short phrase slides up word by word with blur, small sans + the key word in big serif italic ("are going *viral*.", "am I falling *behind?*"), sometimes the key word in condensed caps or big behind the head — this is `mix`. Low third, under the chin, always above the app caption (safe zone).

**Grade & sound.** Slightly darker, cooler, vignette, fine grain; colour keeps one accent per reel. Every effect has a sound: whoosh on words, pop on cards, tick on checks, impact on shakes, ding on chat, riser into charts.

## Russian and other wide scripts

Cyrillic words are ~15% wider: keep `behind` words ≤ 8 letters, prefer the stem noun; the engine shrinks longer ones automatically.
